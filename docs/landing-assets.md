# ClipFlow Landing Assets

## Positioning

ClipFlow is the clipboard manager that lives in your MacBook notch. Hover once to recover recent links, snippets, screenshots, colors, and files without switching apps.

**Headline:** Your clipboard, one hover away.

**Subheadline:** ClipFlow turns the MacBook notch into a fast visual shelf for everything you copy, with local screenshot OCR and privacy controls that keep sensitive content on your Mac.

**Primary CTA:** Download for Mac

**Secondary CTA:** Watch 15 second demo

## Feature Blocks

### Notch-native recall

Open recent clips from the notch while staying in the app you are already using. No Dock switching, no surprise windows.

### Search screenshots by text

Copy a screenshot and ClipFlow extracts text locally, then makes it searchable and copyable like any other clip.

### Privacy by default

Ignore apps, skip secrets, hide sensitive previews, and reveal them only after macOS authentication.

### Smart actions

Clean tracking URLs, copy Markdown links, format JSON, create slugs, and copy OCR text directly from card actions.

## 15 Second Demo Script

1. **0-3s:** Copy a pricing page URL in Arc. Hover the notch. The black shelf expands and the link appears.
2. **3-6s:** Click the card. Show copy feedback, then paste cleanly into Notes or Cursor.
3. **6-10s:** Copy a screenshot with visible text. Hover notch again. Search one word from the screenshot.
4. **10-13s:** Right-click the screenshot card and choose “Copy extracted text”.
5. **13-15s:** Show privacy line: “Local OCR. No cloud clipboard. No Dock clutter.”

## Product Hunt Blurb

ClipFlow is a local-first clipboard manager for macOS that turns the MacBook notch into an instant visual history. Hover to recover recent links, code, screenshots, colors, and files without switching apps. Screenshots get local OCR, so you can search and copy text from images. Sensitive previews stay hidden until you reveal them with macOS authentication.

## Differentiation

- **Versus Raycast clipboard:** more visual, notch-native, focused on copy recovery.
- **Versus Paste:** no subscription-first positioning, local-first privacy, no context switch.
- **Versus Supaste:** stronger MacBook notch integration, local OCR, explicit sensitive-content controls.
- **Versus Maccy:** more visual, screenshot-aware, built for selling/design/dev workflows.
