# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who repeatedly copy text, links, code, screenshots, and files while working across macOS apps. They use ClipFlow in the middle of another task and need quick recall without breaking focus.

## Product Purpose

ClipFlow captures clipboard history, lets users retrieve recent items from the notch or a main library, and gives clear control over what is saved. Success means the app feels invisible until needed, predictable when it appears, and safe for sensitive clipboard content.

## Brand Personality

Fast, quiet, precise. The interface should feel native to macOS, dense enough for power users, and restrained enough to stay out of the way.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard chrome, loud SaaS gradients, modal-heavy flows, unclear clipboard state, and any interaction where clicking the notch unexpectedly opens the main app.

## Design Principles

- Preserve flow: access history without stealing attention from the active app.
- Make capture state explicit: paused, blocked apps, and privacy behavior must be visible.
- Prefer direct actions: copy, paste, favorite, delete, and open main window should have concrete affordances.
- Keep macOS behavior predictable: no Dock presence, no surprise windows, no ambiguous hover/click state.
- Optimize for trust: sensitive clipboard control is a first-class workflow, not a hidden setting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support reduced motion, keyboard shortcuts, visible focus states, readable labels, and non-color-only status indicators.
